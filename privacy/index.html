Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026.

What this policy covers

This policy describes how Melrose Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this website, call our dispatch line or send us an email. This site has no forms. The way to reach us is by phone, and by email for non-urgent questions.

Calls and call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may collect your phone number, your town and the details you give about the water loss so a crew can be arranged. We use this information to respond to your request and to improve how calls are handled.

Email

If you email contact@melrosewaterdamagepros.com, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it to reply to you.

Third-party content on this website

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and an embedded Google map. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads, under their own privacy policies. This site does not run advertising pixels or a customer account system.

Contact about privacy

For privacy questions, email contact@melrosewaterdamagepros.com. We may update this policy from time to time, and the effective date above shows the latest version.
