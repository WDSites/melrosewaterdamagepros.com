Restoration and Mold Services in Melrose, MA

Six services that follow a water loss from the first pump to the final coat of paint. Dispatch is on the 24/7 line.

A water loss rarely needs just one service. A burst pipe starts with removal of standing water, moves to drying, needs cleanup of whatever got soaked and ends with repair. This page lists each service, what it covers and when to call for it, so you can describe your situation accurately on the phone.

Every job begins with a conversation about what the water touched and where it came from. The scope and price are explained before work starts, and same-day response is offered when a crew is available.

Pick the service that matches what you see

Water Damage Repair

Rebuilds ceilings, walls, insulation and floors once the structure is dry.

Flood Damage Restoration

Handles storm runoff, groundwater and sewage backups, usually in basements.

Water Mitigation

Stops the spread and dries the building in the first days after a loss.

Water Damage Cleanup

Removes soaked materials and sanitizes after the water is stopped.

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps and extracts standing water from burst pipes and floods.

Mold Remediation

What happens on a first call

The first call is about understanding the loss, not selling a package.

  1. Describe the lossTell the dispatcher where the water came from, how long it has been wet and what it touched.
  2. Get first-step guidanceYou are told how to stop the source safely and what to leave alone, especially near electrical equipment.
  3. Crew assessmentA crew confirms the damage with moisture meters and explains the scope and price before work starts.
  4. Work in stagesDrying, cleanup and repair are scoped separately so you can see each part.

Price and scope before work starts

We do not post price figures, because the numbers depend on the category of water, the area, the materials, the number of drying days and the rebuild scope. Each service page lists its own cost factors in a table, and the dispatcher or crew explains the real figure before any work begins.

If your loss involves insurance, sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy or ask your insurer. We document each job with photographs and moisture readings, and the published industry standards are ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold.
