When water is spreading in Medford

Spreading water is an emergency in a city where buildings sit close together and basements are common. Call the 24/7 line while the water is still moving. Same-day response is offered when crews are available, and we explain the scope and the price before work starts.

If water has reached an outlet, a panel or a light fixture, stay away and call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk before you call us. For clean water from a split line, water mitigation begins with extraction and moisture mapping.

If water is still spreading, do this in order

These steps keep a small problem from growing before the crew is on the way.

Close the valve

Turn the fixture or main valve closed. If water is coming from outside, skip ahead to protecting your belongings.

Power safely

Switch off wet circuits only if the panel is dry. In a flooded cellar, leave the panel alone and call the utility.

Save what matters

Take documents, devices and photos up to a dry floor, and photograph the room first.

Keep distance

Do not wade through water that came from a drain or the river, and keep children and pets away.

The Mystic River and the flood outlook

The Mystic River Watershed Association predicts that downtown Malden and Medford will flood on average every five years in the future, according to a March 2022 release from Representative Katherine Clark. Medford's mayor said that without interventions, 25 percent of Medford could be affected by flooding within 10 to 20 years.

Those are predictions and a statement by the mayor, not a forecast for your block. Use them as a reason to check the FEMA flood map for your address and to ask your insurer about flood coverage, since standard homeowners policies do not cover flood damage.
