About Melrose Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 dispatch line for water damage and mold in Melrose, MA and the towns around it.

Call (339) 333-9345

Melrose Water Damage Pros is a dispatch service for water damage restoration and mold remediation in Melrose, Massachusetts and the nearby towns of Wakefield, Malden, Stoneham, Saugus and Medford. When you call, a dispatcher listens to what happened, gives first-step safety guidance and sends licensed and insured restoration professionals to look at the job.

We keep this site practical on purpose. It lists the services, explains how drying, cleanup and repair differ, and tells you what to check with your insurer and your city. We do not post invented reviews, ratings or credentials.

How a call is handled

The dispatch line is open 24 hours a day, every day. The dispatcher asks where the water came from, what it touched and whether anyone is near wet electrical equipment, then tells you how to make the situation safer while a crew is arranged.

Same-day response is offered when a crew is available. The scope and price are explained before work starts, and we never promise an arrival time that we cannot control.

If the job involves insurance, the crew photographs the affected areas, logs moisture readings and writes a scope that you can hand to your adjuster. That record is yours to keep, whether or not you file a claim.

What we say and do not say

We explain insurance only in general terms: sudden and accidental water damage is often covered, gradual leaks and neglect often are not, and flood damage usually needs separate coverage. Your policy and your insurer decide, so we tell you to check both.

We name the published industry standards, ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage restoration and S520 for mold remediation, so you can ask any company whether its crews work to them. We do not claim certifications, awards or job counts.

Rooted in Melrose

Melrose is a city of about seven miles north of Boston, shaped by the railroad stops that opened by 1845 and by ponds such as Ell Pond. The neighborhoods we hear from include Downtown Melrose, Melrose Highlands, Cedar Park, East Side, Mount Hood, Oak Grove and Pine Banks, and Wyoming.

Our service pages explain each stage of a water loss, and our service area page lists the nearby towns and their ZIP codes.

What a restoration job covers

Water damage work is a sequence, not a single visit. Emergency water removal pulls out standing water. Water mitigation dries the structure so mold has no chance to settle in, and water damage cleanup takes out soaked materials and sanitizes what remains.

Then water damage repair rebuilds drywall, flooring and paint. For outside water, flood damage restoration applies, and where growth has started, mold remediation contains and removes it. Each is scoped separately so you can see what you are paying for. Neighbors in Wakefield, Malden, Stoneham, Saugus and Medford get the same process.

What we ask you to check on your own

A good restoration company welcomes questions. These are worth asking anyone you call, including us.

  • Whether the company follows ANSI/IICRC S500 for water damage and S520 for mold work.
  • Whether the scope and price are explained in plain terms before any work starts.
  • What the crew will photograph and log, so your insurer has a clear record.
  • Whether a permit is needed for the rebuild, by asking the Melrose Department of Inspectional Services.
  • Who fixes the cause of the leak, since restoration does not stop a failing pipe or roof.

Why a single dispatch line

Most people calling about water damage are stressed, and many do not know the difference between mitigation, cleanup and repair. One number that answers around the clock lets us ask the right questions first, whether the problem is a ceiling stain, a flooded basement or a musty closet, and then point you to the right service page and the right next step.

Our guides in the blog go deeper on ceilings, floors, basements and mold for homeowners who like to read before they call.

Honest limits

We do not waterproof basements, and we do not diagnose illness or promise how a person will feel after mold is removed. Questions about health belong to a physician, and the Massachusetts Department of Public Health homeowner guide on mold is a good starting point for what residents can do themselves before calling a company.

We also do not promise an arrival time. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available, and the dispatcher keeps you informed about the plan while you wait.
