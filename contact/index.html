If anyone is in danger, or if water is near a live electrical panel or a gas appliance, call 911 or the utility first, then call us.

Have these details ready when you call

A few facts help the dispatcher send the right equipment.

  • Where the water came from, such as a pipe, a roof, an appliance, a drain or the outdoors.
  • How long it has been wet, and whether the source has been stopped.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and whether water reached insulation or a ceiling.
  • Whether anything electrical, such as outlets, the panel or appliances, is wet.
  • Your street and town, so the crew can plan the route.

Reaching us

Before you call

If you can do it safely, close the main water valve, lift valuables off wet floors and take a few photographs. Keep clear of wet outlets and the panel. Our emergency water removal page explains what the crew does next.
