Burst pipe water damage cleanup is the work that follows a split supply line: pulling out the water, opening the wall or ceiling the water traveled through, drying the framing and cleaning what it soaked. In Melrose the breaks come from freezing, old fittings, high pressure or a joint that finally let go. Call the 24/7 emergency line as soon as the water is stopped, or while you are still reaching for the shutoff.

A supply line under pressure can soak a room in an hour, and the damage keeps spreading inside the walls after the floor looks dry. Same-day response is offered when a crew is available, and the scope and price are explained before work starts.

Water is spraying or dripping from a pipe and I cannot find the cause. What do I do?

Find the main shutoff and close it, then open one faucet on the lowest floor to let the lines drain. If you cannot reach the valve, call your water utility for help. The city's water comes from the MWRA through about 90 miles of city pipe, and the line inside your house is yours, so the valve at your meter is the one that matters in the first minutes.

Once the water is off, the problem changes from a leak to a wet building. Move rugs and valuables out of the wet zone, take photographs, and call.

First-hour safety steps

These come before any cleanup, and they take a few minutes.

  • Stay out of any room where water is falling through a light fixture or around outlets, and keep children and pets away.
  • Cut power to the wet area only if the panel is dry and you can reach it without standing in water.
  • If you see sparks, smell burning or hear buzzing, leave and call 911 or the utility before you call us.
  • Open a faucet on the lowest floor, lift furniture legs onto foil or blocks and pull up loose rugs.
  • Photograph the pipe, the walls and the waterline before anything is moved.

How the cleanup runs

  1. Extract standing waterSubmersible pumps and wet vacuum wands clear the floor so drying can begin on the structure rather than the puddles.
  2. Find the wet cavitiesThermal imaging and pin-type moisture meters trace where water traveled behind drywall, under cabinets and above ceilings.
  3. Open and removeSmall openings are cut in wall and ceiling board. Soaked insulation and wallboard that cannot dry are bagged and removed.
  4. Dry the framingAir movers and dehumidifiers run until readings on studs, joists and subfloor match dry areas of the house.
  5. Clean and hand offSurfaces are cleaned, the plumber closes the leak, and the rebuild is scoped separately.

Why the ceiling below and the wall beside it matter

Water from an upstairs pipe rarely stays where it started. It runs along joists, drips through light boxes and fills the cavity behind a ceiling until the paint blisters. A ceiling that looks fine can hold water, and a sagging one is heavy enough to fall.

That is why the crew opens the cavity instead of waiting. The Massachusetts Department of Public Health homeowner guide says wet areas should be dried completely within 24 hours and that wallboard and insulation soaked by leaks may have to be discarded. Leaving a wet wall closed turns a plumbing problem into a mold problem.

Insurance and a burst pipe

The Massachusetts Division of Insurance says water damage from causes other than flood, such as a burst pipe, is usually covered when it is sudden and accidental, though the insurer investigates the cause. It also notes that coverage generally depends on the home being properly heated and not left unoccupied. The Division reported that non-flood water and freezing losses made up nearly half of homeowners claims in 2024.

A slow drip behind a wall for months is treated differently from a sudden break. Check your policy or ask your insurer, and keep photographs, the damaged pipe section if the plumber removes it, and a list of what was wet.

Repairs, plumbers and permits

Cleanup stops the damage, but the pipe itself needs a licensed plumber. The city's Department of Inspectional Services lists separate permits for plumbing work, so ask the plumber whether the repair needs one. Ordinary finish repairs, painting and carpeting are exempt from permits.

Once the structure is dry, water damage repair rebuilds drywall, trim and paint. Crews work the same way across Wakefield, Malden and the rest of our service area.

Mistakes to avoid and ways to prevent the next break

The common mistakes are closing the main and then leaving, opening a wet ceiling with a bare hand, running a household vacuum on standing water and painting over a stain before the cavity is dry. Any of these can add days to the job.

To lower the odds of a repeat, learn where your main shutoff is, replace braided washer hoses and old supply fittings before they fail, and keep heat on when you travel in winter. If the break came from cold, see our page on frozen pipe water damage restoration.

When the pipe is fixed and the building is wet, structural drying and dehumidification explains the equipment in detail.

Standing water that already covers a floor is covered in emergency water removal. Crews work to the published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration, and you can ask any company whether theirs do.
